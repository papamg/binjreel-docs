Responsive rules for the builder
Breakpoints 390 / 768 / 1024 / 1440. Below 1024 the desktop hero drops the banner rail and the title scales 76 → 48 → 46px. Poster width steps 118 → 140 → 168 with the shelf always bleeding past the right gutter (never centred, never wrapped). The tab bar becomes the top nav at ≥1024; nothing else moves. The player keeps the vertical video at its native ratio and caps at 772px tall — never crop, never letterbox, never stretch; at <1024 the episode rail becomes the same swipe-up drawer as mobile. The wall is a bottom sheet on mobile and an 860px centred modal on desktop, same three doors in the same order.

Voice note — for judgment calls
binjreel should feel like a premium cinema that happens to be run by funny people. The frame is serious — true black, tight type, generous poster art, nothing wobbly or bubbly — and the wit lives only in the words and in one loud tangerine that shows up when something good is about to happen. When you're unsure: keep the layout calm and let the copy be the joke. Never guilt the viewer, never fake urgency, never a countdown — the paywall is a menu of three good options and the door out is always visible. Free and earned things are lime; things you pay for are tangerine; the coin is the only gold. If a screen feels like it's shouting, take away a color, not a joke.
