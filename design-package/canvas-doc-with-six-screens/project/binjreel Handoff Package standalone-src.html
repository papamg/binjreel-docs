BUILD CONTRACT · v1.0 · FINAL
binjreel design package
Two locked themes — Dark (5a) and Light (6a) — across the consumer app (mobile 390 + web 1440) and the admin backend (web 1440). Every value here is final and literal. Where a screen appears in one theme only, the note says which rules carry over.
--act · TANGERINE
Money & intent
Primary CTA, subscription door, progress, active tab, active chip, focus ring. The thing you tap.
--free · LIME
Free & earned
FREE / NEW badges, the rewarded-ad door, "no coins", unlocked-episode cells, success toasts.
--heat · PINK
Heat & the crowd
HOT, rank 1–2, HA-meter, ha-counts, LIVE, unread dot. Never a button. Never on the paywall.
NON-NEGOTIABLE RULES
01  Pink never appears on the wall, in a price, or on a button. Heat beside a price is pressure.
02  No guilt copy, no countdowns, no "you'll lose your streak". The dismiss affordance is always visible.
03  In light mode nothing but a filled badge or a progress bar sits on poster art. Titles go below, in --text-primary.
04  The player is dark in both themes. Light chrome around lit video reads as a bug.
05  Accent fills keep their bright value in both themes; only accent text switches to its dark twin.
06  IA is fixed: five tabs, three wall doors, four episode lock states. Restyle, don't re-architect.

7 · Voice note
FOR THE BUILDER'S JUDGMENT CALLS
binjreel is a premium cinema that happens to be run by funny people. The frame is serious — true black or clean paper, tight type, generous poster art, nothing wobbly or bubbly — and the wit lives only in the words and in the colour. Three accents, three jobs, and they never trade places: tangerine is what you tap, lime is what costs nothing, pink is what other people are doing. When you're unsure, keep the layout calm and let the copy be the joke. Never guilt the viewer, never fake urgency, never a countdown — the paywall is a menu of three good options and the door out is always visible. If a screen feels like it's shouting, take away a colour, not a joke.
COPY RULES
Sentence case everywhere except badges and overlines. Contractions always. One joke per screen, in the smallest text that can carry it. Numbers are facts, never bait.
WHEN IN DOUBT
Follow the mobile spec and scale up, not the reverse. Poster art is the only guaranteed image — never design a layout that breaks without a photo.
ASK, DON'T GUESS
New surfaces not in this package — rewards hub, profile, search results, onboarding — need a design pass before build. Don't extrapolate a new pattern from these tokens alone.
