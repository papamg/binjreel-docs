## The one-liner

**binjreel is the vertical micro-series platform for every genre the $3-billion short-drama
boom left on the table — launching with comedy, on a payments-and-access engine that is already
built, tested, and audited.**

## The moment

Short-form vertical series went from novelty to the fastest-rising category in mobile
entertainment in three years: roughly **$3B in in-app revenue in 2025, up 115% year over
year**, on a market estimated at **$14B and forecast to reach $26B by 2030**. In the US — 60%
of global revenue — viewers of the category leader spend **more time per day in a vertical
drama app than in Netflix, Prime Video, or Disney+ mobile**.

And nearly all of it is *one genre for one audience*: billionaire-romance melodrama. The
industry itself has named the next frontier — Hollywood-backed entrants (MicroCo, Crisp,
GammaTime, vertTV) are all raising money right now to push into comedy, documentary, and
broader genres. **None of them owns it yet. That is the open lane binjreel launches into.**

## Why comedy wins the vertical format

Comedy is the most naturally *shareable* genre in existence — every punchline is a clip, every
clip is an ad someone sends a friend for free. It's rewatchable, sound-on, culture-making, and
underserved: the format's proven mechanics (60–120-second episodes, cliff-beats, free run then
a wall) map perfectly onto set-up/punchline structure. binjreel launches with **two original
comedy series from Journey Viral (in production now)** and a roadmap of all genres —
including **micro-docs**, the format's untouched nonfiction frontier.

## What's already real (not a deck promise)

Most entrants build the show and fake the store. binjreel built the store first, properly:

- **A payments-and-access core that is built, tested, and security-audited** — an append-only,
  double-entry coin ledger; store-receipt verification (Apple & Google server-to-server);
  an entitlement engine that is the only path to a playable stream. Proven by test: a replayed
  payment can never double-credit, a race can never overdraw, an unentitled request never
  yields a video URL.
- **Real paywalled streaming, live-verified**: masters in owned storage, CDN transcode and
  delivery, signed expiring playback links proven end-to-end (an expired link is dead at the
  edge — demonstrated, not asserted).
- **Two-plane economics**: we own the control plane (the IP); video delivery is rented per-GB
  and vendor-swappable. Infrastructure runs lean enough for a one-person-plus-agents operation
  today, and scales in named stages.

## The unfair advantages

1. **Funnel science, not funnel spend.** The category leader grossed ~$1.2B in 2025 *and still
   lost money* — customer acquisition by brute-force ad buying. binjreel's sister platform
   **JVScope** attributes the funnel scientifically: which clip, on which platform, produces
   installs — and which installs become *payers*. Every share out of binjreel carries a tagged
   origin; every dollar in traces back to the hook that earned it. We buy attention like a
   quant, not a whale.
2. **The honest wall.** The category's app-store reviews repeat the same wounds: charged twice,
   charged for what was already watched, coins that vanish. binjreel's ledger makes those
   failures *architecturally impossible* — and we market that promise: never charged twice,
   purchases follow you forever, every expiry visible before it bites. In a category built on
   dark patterns, trust is a growth feature.
3. **Platform, not just app.** binjreel is multi-tenant from the data layer up, with books
   that reconcile to the penny by construction. Phase two invites other studios onto the rails
   — revenue-share statements generated from an audit-grade ledger. The incumbents are studios
   with apps; binjreel is infrastructure with a flagship.
4. **Full-funnel product.** Instant web playback for anyone who taps a clip (no install cliff),
   SEO'd show pages, then a native app where the three-legged economy lives. The install is
   earned by the content, not demanded before the first laugh.

## The business model (the category's proven stool, all three legs)

**Coins** per episode after a free run · **Subscription** (ad-free, HD, offline, early
release) · **Rewards economy** (daily check-ins, streaks, rewarded ads, referral) that
monetizes non-payers, builds the daily habit, and feeds the wall. Complete series monetize at
$15–50 per paying viewer across the category; web top-up adds a store-fee-free lane. All
prices, free-episode counts, and reward values are live config — tuned on real funnel data,
not app-store release cycles.
